Collected hourly from thirteen national titles’ own feeds. Stories are found by clustering, not by a fixed list of topics, so the shapes change as the news does. Each article is scored on its headline and standfirst against an explicit target — what a favourable score would be favourable towards — because “net zero rollback” scored as though it meant net zero puts half the press on the wrong side of zero.

Two papers, shown in amber above, reach us through Google rather than their own feeds. Those carry no front-page position and no standfirst, so their items are scored on the headline alone and count for less.

Sport, showbiz and puzzles are removed before anything is counted — about a fifth of what the papers publish, and very unevenly split between them.
